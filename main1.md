# RetailGuard - Customer Retention Case Study
## Business problem
Retail customer retention and revenue are declining. The objective is to understand why customers stop purchasing and suggest practical retention actions.
## Questions to investigate
- What does customer inactivity look like?
- Which factors may influence repeat purchases?
- What loyalty practices are used by retailers?
## Analysis approach
1. Review publicly available retail case studies and datasets.
2. List measurable churn indicators (e.g., days since last purchase, fewer visits, lower spend).
3. Separate observed facts from assumptions.
4. Compare retention practices such as loyalty points, personalized offers and service recovery.
